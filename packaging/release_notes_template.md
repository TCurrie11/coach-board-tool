Draw lacrosse plays, animate them step by step, and export a video you can drop straight into PowerPoint or Keynote.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| **Mac** (M1 or newer) | `CoachBoardTool-macos-appleSilicon.zip` |
| **Windows** | `CoachBoardTool-windows.exe` |

Scroll down to **Assets** and click the file for your computer.

### First launch

**Mac:** double-click the zip to unzip it, then open **READ ME FIRST.txt** in the folder. The first time, macOS blocks apps from independent developers. Allow it once in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. The guide walks you through it.

**Windows:** the first time, Windows shows a blue *"Windows protected your PC"* message. Click **More info** → **Run anyway**.

You only need to do this once.
